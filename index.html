Privacy Policy — WorkLabs

This Privacy Policy explains how WorkLabs (“we”, “our”) collects, uses, and stores information when you use the WorkLabs mobile application (the “App”).

The App is an HR / workforce tool provided to organisations that subscribe to WorkLabs (each a “Customer”). It is not a consumer social app and we do not show ads.

Employees use the App under their employer’s WorkLabs account. Your employer is responsible for HR records in that account. WorkLabs processes that data to run the App for the Customer.

1. Who we are

WorkLabs
Contact: kumarrahul27817@gmail.com
Address: India. For postal correspondence, email the contact above.

2. Information we collect

Account and login

  • Work email address
  • Password (sent securely to our servers to sign you in; we do not store your password on the phone)
  • After login: name, employee code, role, branch, and profile photo if your employer’s HR record has one. These HR profile fields belong to your employer’s WorkLabs account.

Location

  • Approximate and precise location (GPS / network)
  • Used only to confirm you are at an authorised office geofence when you check in or check out
  • Location is requested while you use the App (foreground). We do not use background tracking for ads or marketing
  • If you deny location permission, you cannot mark attendance from the App; other HR screens may still work

Attendance and HR data

  • Check-in / check-out time, GPS coordinates of the punch, matched office location
  • Leave, attendance history, payroll summary, team attendance (if your role allows)

On the device

  • Login tokens and a short session log (so the App can stay signed in and diagnose logout issues)
  • This stays on your phone until you log out or uninstall the App

3. How we use information

  • Authenticate you
  • Record and verify attendance
  • Show your profile, leave, payroll, and (for managers) team information
  • Secure the App and fix technical issues

We do not sell your data. We do not use location or email for advertising.

4. Legal basis (where applicable)

Employment / legitimate interest to run attendance and HR, and your consent for location permission on the device.

5. Sharing

Data is processed on WorkLabs servers and hosting used to run the App for your employer. We do not share it with advertisers. We may share it if required by law, or with IT vendors who host the system under our instructions. Your employer’s authorised HR/IT users can access records in their organisation.

6. Retention

  • Login tokens: until logout or expiry
  • Attendance and HR records: as required by your employer’s company / labour policy
  • Device session logs: limited recent events on the phone

7. Security

Access uses HTTPS. Tokens are stored on the device. Only authorised WorkLabs staff and your employer’s HR/IT users can access the relevant server records. No system is 100% secure.

8. Your choices

  • Deny or revoke location in Android Settings → Apps → WorkLabs → Permissions
  • Change password in the App
  • Log out to clear tokens on the device
  • Uninstall the App to remove local data
  • For correction or deletion of HR records, contact your employer’s HR department
  • For App or privacy questions about WorkLabs, email kumarrahul27817@gmail.com

9. Children

The App is for employees only, not for children under 13 (or 16 where required).

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The new date will appear at the top. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.
